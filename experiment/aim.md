### Aim of the Experiment

1. **To emulate and understand** the electronic analog signal conditioning chain of a dual-wavelength transmission pulse oximeter, from photodiode current transduction to transimpedance amplification, synchronous demodulation, active bandpass filtering, and analog-to-digital conversion (ADC).
2. **To study the photophysical principles** of the Beer-Lambert law and differential optical absorption of Oxyhemoglobin ($\text{HbO}_2$) and Deoxyhemoglobin ($\text{Hb}$) at Red ($660\text{ nm}$) and Infrared ($940\text{ nm}$) wavelengths.
3. **To synthesize and analyze** physiological photoplethysmogram (PPG) waveforms, including systolic ejection peaks, dicrotic notches, diastolic runoff, and the Perfusion Index ($PI\%$).
4. **To perform empirical calibration** using a 9-step guided workflow, calculating the Modulation Ratio $R = \frac{(AC/DC)_{660}}{(AC/DC)_{940}}$, fitting linear and polynomial calibration curves ($\text{SpO}_2 = A - B \cdot R$), and evaluating absolute error and percentage-point difference against simulated gold-standard co-oximetry references.
5. **To explore 3D biomedical instrumentation** interactively via a WebGL 3D virtual reality workspace featuring sensor clips, LED optical cones, tissue perfusion, and PCB circuit blocks.
